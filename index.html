Settings & backup

Data safety

Everything you do is saved automatically to this browser as you go — nothing to remember. But that storage lives only on this device, so it's worth keeping a backup file, especially before switching phones or clearing browser data.

Progress-sharing reminder

If you want a nudge to send your log to someone (accountability partner, therapist), pick a cadence — a soft, dismissible reminder will show up in the app, never a notification you have to act on.

Install on your phone

Open the browser menu (⋮) and tap "Install app" or "Add to Home screen." Once installed it opens full-screen and works offline — your data still lives on this device.
